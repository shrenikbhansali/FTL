## 5) Analysis & conclusions

Key observations from non‑GSM8K evals (hellaswag/xsum/hotpotqa/mbpp):
- Centralized metrics are identical across tags (as expected; same centralized config).
- Participation increase (ta_participation_mid, sample_rate=0.35) strengthens FedAvg and weakens SubspaceBank on HellaSwag and XSum — consistent with FedAvg behaving more like centralized under higher participation.
- Projection strength (rho=1 vs rho=0.5 vs 0.0) shows no measurable difference in this sweep (ta_rho05 vs ta_rho0 identical), implying global metrics are insensitive to rho under task‑aware projection at current budgets.
- Increasing shared rank (ta_rglobal2) improves bank_perclient on XSum relative to FedAvg (slight positive), but reduces HellaSwag gain compared to ta_rho05/ta_rho0.
- Reweighting shift (ta_beta50) keeps strong HellaSwag gains but hurts XSum/HotPotQA vs FedAvg; aggressive residual shrink appears to trade transfer for specificity.
- MBPP remains near zero in all configs at 100‑sample eval; no clear signal yet.

Overall: task‑aware projection + task‑balanced shared basis improved HellaSwag gains for SubspaceBank but still trades off XSum/HotPotQA; the best bank‑over‑fedavg gap is achieved with low participation and stronger shared weighting, while XSum appears to benefit from larger shared rank.
